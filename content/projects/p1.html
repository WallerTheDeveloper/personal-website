Pick Ma Job is a product I designed, built and run myself. It watches the freelance job boards — Upwork and LinkedIn — reads every new posting as it appears, and works out which ones are genuinely worth answering before you ever see them.

It is live at pickmajob.cc, a small group of freelancers use it, and I use it myself every day.

The problem it solves

Freelance work goes to whoever is early and relevant. The price of that is reading: hundreds of new postings a week, of which perhaps three are a real fit. I was spending hours every week on that reading — and the good ones still slipped past while I was asleep.

Pick Ma Job does the reading. What arrives is a short list, ranked, with a reason attached to every entry.

What it does

◈ Reads everything, constantly

New postings are collected from Upwork and LinkedIn around the clock, so nothing is missed for having been published at three in the morning.

◎ Scores the fit

Every posting is judged against your uploaded CV and your stated preferences, then scored out of ten. Anything under your threshold never reaches you.

⟐ Explains its verdict

Strong matches get a second, deeper read: what lines up, what does not, and what to be careful of. You decide with the reasoning in front of you rather than a bare number.

⤢ Writes the first draft

One click produces a CV tailored to that particular job and a proposal written for that particular client. Editing a draft is a very different task from facing a blank page.

⊘ Remembers your no

Blacklist a company and it never appears again. Rule out the kinds of work you do not take and those postings stop arriving. Repeats between runs are dropped automatically.

▤ Keeps the shortlist tidy

Drag jobs into lists of your own, and filter by platform, score or status. The pile stays a pile; what you see is the decision.

How it works, in four steps

  1. You say who you are. Upload your CV as a PDF and describe the work you want. That becomes the yardstick every posting is measured against.
  2. It goes and looks. A run gathers the latest postings from the platforms you have switched on, then discards duplicates, blacklisted companies and anything matching the words you have excluded.
  3. It reads, then reads harder. Everything surviving that gets a quick score; only the ones clearing your threshold get the slow, detailed read. Two passes instead of one is what keeps a run cheap enough to leave running.
  4. You get a shortlist. Ranked, each with its reasoning, ready to keep, dismiss, or answer with a generated CV and proposal.

Trusted with your CV

There are no passwords to lose: signing in sends a one-time link to your email address. Every account sees only its own jobs, lists and documents — that separation is enforced at the database, not left to the screen you happen to be on.

Status — live at pickmajob.cc and in daily use. A handful of other freelancers are on it alongside me, and it is still under active development.

Under the hood

Built solo, front to back — the interface, the engine behind it, the database, the server it runs on, and the tests that keep it honest.
